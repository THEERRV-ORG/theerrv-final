For years, Nallathae Nadakkum was doing extraordinary work with almost no digital presence. The trust feeds the elderly in shelters and on the roadside, arranges dignified last rites, runs an ambulance service for the poor, rescues and rehouses elderly people, and supports students — all coordinated locally, by phone, by hand, and through the founder's own social media. The mission was strong. The way to reach and grow it was the missing piece.

At THEERRV, we saw the same challenges that hold back many purpose-driven organisations:

- No single place to explain the mission
- Donors with no easy way to give
- Services scattered, not clearly listed
- Volunteers with no way to sign up
- Legal and transparency details hard to find

A purpose-built website solves this by bringing the entire mission — services, impact, donations, and volunteers — into one trusted platform.

## What we set out to build

The goal was a fast, lightweight website with a proper database behind it — clean, easy to maintain, and effortless for a first-time visitor to use. We designed it around how people actually decide to help: they want to understand the cause, see the proof, and give in seconds.

Here's what the platform includes:

- **A living home page** — a hero showcase that cycles through real images of the trust's work, with live counters showing reach and social action, and donation details front and centre
- **An about page that builds trust** — the story of the trust, a dated journey map of its milestones, and the founder's profile alongside his awards
- **Activities and gallery** — every event documented with photos, dates, and details
- **A clear services page** — all the trust's work in one place
- **A community and support page** — where visitors can request help, donate, or volunteer through simple forms

## The feature that makes giving effortless

Most donation forms ask people to type too much, and people drop off. We flipped it. A visitor simply selects the service they want to support — food donation, dignified last rites, student support, ambulance fuel, blood donation, or material aid — and the form auto-fills the relevant details. They only add their name and a few basics, with validation guiding them the whole way.

On submit, two things happen at once. The request is saved to a Firestore database and appears instantly on the trust's admin page. At the same time, WhatsApp opens with a message already written to the trust's number — the donor just taps send.

Imagine a mission where:

- ✅ Anyone can understand the cause in one visit
- ✅ Donating takes seconds, not paperwork
- ✅ Every request lands in one admin dashboard
- ✅ Volunteers sign up without a single phone call
- ✅ Transparency and legal proof are one click away

> Technology, done right, doesn't sit between a person and a good cause — it removes everything in the way.

## Built for trust and for giving

Because credibility matters most for a charitable trust, we gave transparency and legal documents their own dedicated section. The contact page carries a UPI scanner for instant donations, the trust's bank details, a contact form, and the exact location on Google Maps. And because the founder is genuinely active online — sharing his work on YouTube, Instagram, and WhatsApp — we highlighted those channels throughout, so his existing audience flows straight into the site.

## How we approached the build

We kept the engineering deliberate: a lightweight static front end for speed, a Firestore database for storing and surfacing requests, and an admin page so the trust can see everything in one view. Every decision followed sound coding principles and was made to be durable and easy to maintain — a platform the trust can rely on and grow into, not a one-off.

## The outcome

The founder was thrilled with the result — so much so that he chose to showcase the website on Independence Day. For a trust that had run entirely on the ground until then, having a home online that matches the scale of its heart was a genuine milestone.
